#  Uber Data Analysis Project

> **An end-to-end Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) and Feature Engineering project to uncover passenger behaviors, peak demand trends, and operational insights for Uber.**

---

###  Project Overview
This project analyzes real-world Uber trip data to help optimize services and drive data-backed business decisions. By performing thorough data manipulation and visualization, we answer critical operational questions required by the company.

---

###  Key Business Insights Discovered
Through this analysis, we solved the following core business questions:

* ** Top Booking Category:** **Business rides** dominate the dataset heavily (1,061 bookings) compared to Personal travel (75 bookings).
* ** Primary Purpose:** Excluding missing values, **Meetings** and **Meal/Entertainment** are the top reasons for booking a ride.
* ** Peak Hours:** Cab bookings reach their maximum during the **Afternoon** hours, followed closely by the Evening.
* ** Low-Demand Months:** Discovered specific seasonal trends and months where Uber rides are requested less frequently.
* ** Peak Days:** **Friday** is the busiest day of the week (204 bookings), while Sunday sees the lowest demand.
* ** Average Trip Distance:** Most trips are very short, heavily concentrated between **0 to 10 miles**, with a massive peak around **2–5 miles**.
